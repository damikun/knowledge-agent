# Getting started

Knowledge Agent answers your customers from your own content, on your website and in the portal, and hands them to a person when they need one. The setup has four steps, and Home shows them with a checklist until they are done: [Home](/home).

## The four setup steps

1. Connect a model: add an API key on [AI models](/models#providers), so the agent can answer and read your sources.
2. Add knowledge: a website, files or text on [Sources](/sources).
3. Create an agent: give it a persona, the knowledge it may use and its tools on [Agents](/agents/new).
4. Put a widget on your site: create a widget for the agent and paste its snippet into your site on [Widgets](/widgets/new).

The widget step counts as done after its first conversation, because that shows the snippet is on a live page.

## How the parts fit

- Connections give access, set up once: AI providers on [AI models](/models), tools from MCP servers on [Tools](/tools), and email and Slack on [Notifications](/notifications).
- An agent decides and answers: its knowledge, tone, tools and when to offer a person ([Agents](/agents)).
- A channel is where people meet the agent. Today that is a website widget ([Widgets](/widgets)): how it looks, which sites may show it, and where requests for a person go.

Your team then works in the [Inbox](/inbox), [Contacts](/contacts) and [Feedback](/feedback), over the conversations of every widget.

## Admins and support users

- Admins see every page and change every setting.
- Support users work in the [Inbox](/inbox), [Contacts](/contacts) and [Feedback](/feedback). They can answer visitors, assign and label conversations and edit contacts, but they cannot change settings.

## Try the agent before visitors do

Open the [Playground](/playground) and ask what your visitors would ask. Pick a widget there to test exactly what its visitors get. Playground chats are tests: they count nowhere and never reach the Inbox.

## The help assistant

The question mark in the header opens this help, on every page. Press ? to open it from the keyboard. It knows which page you are on. It needs a chat model: without one it says so and links [AI models](/models). With a model it writes answers with links to these pages. It reads only this help, never your sources or conversations, and it keeps nothing: closing the browser tab forgets the chat. An admin can turn it off for everyone under Settings > System ([System](/system)).
