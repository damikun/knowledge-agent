# Labels, contacts and segments

Labels sort conversations by topic, and segments are saved filters of contacts. Both help your team find what matters and show up in [Analytics](/analytics).

## Labels on conversations

- Staff set labels in a conversation's details in the [Inbox](/inbox), and filter the list by a label.
- Admins create, rename and delete labels from the label filter. Each label has a name, a color and a meaning.
- A label set to apply automatically is chosen by the utility model when a website conversation closes, at most two per conversation. A label staff remove stays removed.
- Re-label runs the automatic labels again for recent conversations, for example after you add a label.

Analytics counts conversations per label against the period before ([Analytics](/analytics)).

## Contacts

Every website conversation belongs to a contact on [Contacts](/contacts): anonymous like "Visitor 4F2A" until the visitor leaves a name or email, or your site identifies them. Staff edit a contact's name, email and notes. Admins export everything about a contact or erase it with all its conversations.

## Attributes

Attributes are facts your website sends about a contact, such as plan or seats. The Attributes tab on [Contacts](/contacts) lists every key with its type, who may set it, and whether the agent may see it. A value from the browser never overwrites one from your server or an admin.

## Segments

1. Open [Contacts](/contacts) and add filters, such as plan is pro and last seen within 30 days.
2. Press Save as segment and give it a name.

Whoever matches the filters now is in the segment. Use segments to filter contacts and conversations, and see their conversations, handover rate and rating on [Analytics](/analytics).
