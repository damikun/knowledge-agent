# Travel and Expense Policy

**Policy ID:** FIN-POL-03 · **Version:** 5.1 · **Owner:** Finance · **In force since:** 1 January 2026, with the amendments in section 10 effective 1 April 2026 · **Travel desk:** travel@orbitwell.example

This policy covers business travel and work-related expenses for all Orbitwell Robotics employees. All trips are booked and all expenses are claimed in **Faretrail**, our travel and expense tool (travel.orbitwell.example). Spend company money as carefully as you would spend your own, and choose the option that is cheapest overall once working time is taken into account.

## 1. Before you travel

- Every business trip must be pre-approved by your line manager in Faretrail **before** anything is booked.
- Trips outside Europe additionally require approval from your department director.
- Book flights at least 21 days before departure where possible; late bookings need a short justification.
- Orbitwell's business travel insurance automatically covers every approved trip. The policy certificate can be downloaded from your Faretrail trip page.

## 2. Transport

### 2.1 Flights

Cabin class depends on the scheduled flight time of each leg:

| Scheduled flight time per leg | Permitted class |
|---|---|
| Up to 6 hours | Economy |
| More than 6 hours, up to 11 hours | Premium economy |
| More than 11 hours | Business class, with director approval |

One checked bag is reimbursable. Extra baggage for demo equipment or tools is reimbursable when noted in the trip request.

### 2.2 Rail

Second class is the standard. First class is permitted for journeys longer than 2.5 hours so that you can work on the train. See section 10 for the rail-first rule introduced on 1 April 2026.

### 2.3 Private car

Using your own car requires prior approval and is only allowed when it is cheaper than rail or a rental car. The mileage rate is **€0.32 per km**, plus motorway tolls and parking. The rate covers fuel, wear and your own insurance; Orbitwell does not reimburse vignettes for private vehicles beyond the trip period.

### 2.4 Rental cars

Book compact or intermediate class through Faretrail and return the car refuelled. Larger vehicles are allowed only when transporting equipment or four or more colleagues.

## 3. Accommodation

Hotel caps apply per room and night, including VAT and local city taxes:

| City tier | Hotel cap per night |
|---|---|
| Tier 1 | €215 |
| Tier 2 | €160 |
| Tier 3 | €115 |
| Domestic trips in the Czech Republic | €95 |

City tiers are listed in section 4. Stays above the cap, for example during trade fairs, need a justification in the trip request. Private holiday rentals are not permitted.

## 4. Per diem

The per diem covers meals, local public transport within the destination city and tips. It is paid per calendar day of travel.

| City tier | Example cities | Per diem |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | London, Zurich, Geneva, Paris, Oslo, Copenhagen | €76 |
| Tier 2 | Munich, Vienna, Amsterdam, Brussels, Stockholm, Milan, Lyon | €59 |
| Tier 3 | Bratislava, Warsaw, Wrocław, Kraków, Budapest, Lisbon, Ljubljana | €43 |
| Domestic | Any destination in the Czech Republic, trips longer than 5 hours | €19 |

- Cities that are not listed are classified by the travel desk; until then Tier 2 applies.
- Travel days shorter than 8 hours receive **60%** of the per diem.
- If a meal is provided by a customer, a conference or the hotel rate, reduce the per diem by 15% for breakfast, 35% for lunch and 35% for dinner.

## 5. Expense reports and receipts

- Submit your expense report in Faretrail within **30 calendar days** of the end of the trip (for expenses not linked to a trip, within 30 calendar days of the purchase).
- An itemised receipt is required for every expense of €10 or more. Photos taken with the Faretrail mobile app are accepted; keep the originals until the report has been paid.
- Reports submitted more than 90 days after the trip are not reimbursed unless your director grants a written exception.
- Approved reports are paid with the next monthly payroll if the final approval is given by the 20th of the month.

### 5.1 Approval thresholds

| Report total | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to €1,200 | Line manager |
| €1,200.01 to €5,000 | Department director |
| Above €5,000 | Head of Finance |

Nobody may approve their own expenses. Reports from directors are approved by the Head of Finance regardless of the amount.

## 6. Corporate card

- The Orbitwell Corporate Card is issued to employees who travel regularly, once they have **successfully completed their probation period** (the length of probation is set out in the Employee Handbook). Until then, pay with your own card and claim the expenses in Faretrail.
- The standard monthly limit is **€3,500**. Temporary increases for trade fairs or long customer installations can be requested from the travel desk.
- Cash withdrawals are limited to €120 per trip and only where cards are not accepted.
- Personal use is not allowed. Reconcile every card transaction in Faretrail by the 10th of the following month.
- Report a lost or stolen card immediately to the hotline printed on the card and to travel@orbitwell.example.

## 7. Client entertainment

Business meals with customers or partners are reimbursable up to **€85 per person**, including drinks. List all attendees, their companies and the business purpose in the report. Events with more than six external guests require director pre-approval.

## 8. Non-reimbursable items

The following are never reimbursed:

- Minibar items and in-room entertainment
- In-flight internet access, unless required for urgent work and noted in the report
- Traffic, parking and speeding fines
- Seat upgrades, lounge access and loyalty programme fees
- Spa, gym and leisure activities
- Personal travel insurance (company insurance already covers approved trips)
- Laundry on trips shorter than five nights
- Alcohol, except as part of approved client entertainment
- Home-office equipment and furniture (covered by the home-office allowance described in the Employee Handbook)
- Extra hotel nights, side trips or other costs of a private extension to a business trip

## 9. Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Traveller | Books through Faretrail, keeps receipts, submits reports on time |
| Line manager | Pre-approves trips, checks reports for business purpose |
| Department director | Approves long-haul and higher-value spend, grants exceptions |
| Finance | Audits a sample of reports every month, maintains this policy |

## 10. Changes effective 1 April 2026

1. **Rail first.** For destinations reachable by train in **4.5 hours** or less (for example Brno–Vienna, Brno–Prague, Brno–Bratislava and Brno–Budapest), rail is the default. A flight on these routes requires a written justification and director approval.
2. **Taxis and ride-hailing** are reimbursed in standard class only. For airport transfers, use public transport or the airport shuttle where one is available.
3. **Automatic per diems.** Per diems are now calculated from the approved Faretrail itinerary and paid through payroll in the month after the trip, so they no longer need to be claimed in the expense report.
4. **Hotels through Faretrail only.** Hotels booked outside Faretrail require prior approval from the travel desk and are reimbursed only up to the applicable cap.
5. **Travel emissions.** Faretrail now shows an estimated CO2 figure for every itinerary, and each department receives a quarterly travel emissions report.

Questions about this policy go to travel@orbitwell.example.
