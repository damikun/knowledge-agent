# Q2 2026 Quarterly Business Update

**Period:** 1 April – 30 June 2026 · **Published:** 28 July 2026 · **Classification:** INTERNAL · **Prepared by:** Finance and Strategy

Orbitwell Robotics delivered its strongest quarter so far. Demand for collaborative automation in machine tending and assembly remained high across Europe, and Orbitwell Fleet continued to grow faster than hardware. This update summarises our results, the state of our products and teams, and our outlook for the rest of the year.

## 1. Highlights

- **Revenue of €48.6 million**, up **21%** year over year (Q2 2025: €40.2 million).
- **1,940 robot arms shipped**, up 17% year over year.
- **Gross margin of 41.8%**, an improvement of 2.2 percentage points.
- **11,200 robots connected to Orbitwell Fleet** at the end of June, 2,100 more than at the end of March.
- **Headcount of 612** employees on 30 June 2026.
- **New office in Bilbao, Spain**, opening in October 2026.

## 2. Financial summary

| Metric | Q2 2026 | Q2 2025 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | €48.6 million | €40.2 million | +21% |
| Hardware revenue | €36.9 million | €31.4 million | +18% |
| Services and Fleet subscriptions | €11.7 million | €8.8 million | +33% |
| Gross margin | 41.8% | 39.6% | +2.2 pp |
| EBITDA | €6.3 million | €4.1 million | +54% |
| Robot arms shipped | 1,940 | 1,658 | +17% |

Revenue for the first half of 2026 reached €90.1 million (Q1 2026: €41.5 million). Recurring Fleet subscription revenue was €5.9 million in the quarter and now accounts for half of our services revenue. The margin improvement came mainly from a better product mix, lower freight costs and higher utilisation of the Brno assembly hall.

Central and Eastern Europe was the fastest-growing region, with revenue up 34% year over year, driven by automotive suppliers in the Czech Republic, Slovakia and Poland. Southern Europe is still our smallest European region, but the order pipeline in Spain and Portugal more than doubled during the quarter, which is why we are opening a local office there.

## 4. Products

**OW-X7.** The OW-X7 collaborative arm accounted for 1,310 of the 1,940 units shipped. The remaining units were legacy compact arms and service replacement arms. Firmware 3.4.0 for the OW-X7 was released in June with new motion features requested by machine-tending customers, and customers report shorter cycle times after upgrading.

**Orbitwell Fleet.** Version 2 of the Fleet API, generally available since February, is now used by 140 customers to dispatch jobs and stream telemetry into their own production systems. Fleet platform availability in Q2 was **99.98%**. The Fleet Insights analytics add-on is in beta with twelve customers and is planned for general release in Q4 2026.

**Support.** The number of customers on the Premier support tier grew from 51 to 64 during the quarter. Most new Premier customers run more than twenty robots in multi-shift operation.

## 5. People and offices

- Headcount grew from 571 at the end of March to **612** on 30 June 2026. Most new colleagues joined R&D and field service.
- Our offices are in Brno (headquarters, R&D and assembly), Wrocław (service and refurbishment centre) and Lyon (sales and application centre).
- **Bilbao:** our new office in Bilbao, Spain, opens in **October 2026**. It will be the sales and service hub for Spain and Portugal, start with an initial team of 18 and include a customer demo cell.
- A second assembly line in Brno was commissioned in May, raising annual assembly capacity to 9,000 arms.

## 6. Outlook

- We are raising our **full-year 2026 revenue guidance to €190–198 million** (previously €182–190 million).
- The third quarter is usually affected by the August holiday season in our main markets, so we expect revenue slightly below Q2.
- We plan to hire about 70 additional employees in the second half of the year, mainly for Bilbao and R&D.
- The main risks remain lead times for servo drive components and movements in the euro–koruna exchange rate.
