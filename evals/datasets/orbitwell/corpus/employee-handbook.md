# Orbitwell Robotics Employee Handbook

**Edition:** 2026 · **Owner:** People & Culture · **Applies to:** all employees with an Orbitwell employment contract (local law prevails where it is more favourable) · **Questions:** hr@orbitwell.example

This handbook explains the everyday rules for working at Orbitwell Robotics: when we work, how time off is earned and taken, and what happens when life gets in the way. Requests, balances and approvals all live in **People Hub** (people.orbitwell.example). If something in this handbook is unclear, ask your manager or your People Partner before you act on an assumption.

## 1. Working hours

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Standard working week | 37.5 hours, Monday to Friday (7.5 hours per day) |
| Flexitime window | Start between 07:00 and 10:00, finish no later than 19:00 |
| Core hours (Monday–Thursday) | 10:00–15:00 |
| Core hours (Friday) | 10:00–13:00 |
| Lunch break | Half an hour, unpaid, taken between 11:00 and 14:00 |
| Shift roles (assembly, refurbishment) | Two shifts: 06:00–14:00 and 14:00–22:00, published on the monthly roster |

During core hours everyone is expected to be available for meetings, whether in the office or working remotely. Record your working time in People Hub by the last working day of each month.

**Overtime** must be approved by your manager before it is worked. It is compensated with time off in lieu, to be taken within three months, or paid with the statutory overtime premium if time off is not possible. Ordered overtime is capped at 150 hours per calendar year.

## 2. Vacation

### 2.1 Accrual

Vacation accrues monthly from your first day of employment, including during probation.

| Length of service | Annual vacation | Monthly accrual |
|---|---|---|
| Years 1 to 4 | 22 days | 1.83 days |
| From the first day of year 5 | 27 days | 2.25 days |

Part-time employees accrue vacation pro rata to their contracted hours. Your current balance, including accrued but untaken days, is shown on the People Hub home page.

### 2.2 Requesting vacation

- Request every absence in People Hub; your manager approves or declines within three working days.
- For absences of five or more consecutive working days, submit the request at least **2 weeks in advance**.
- Plan at least one continuous block of two weeks per year so that you genuinely switch off.
- Teams with customer-facing rotas (support, field service) publish a summer holiday plan by 15 May each year.

### 2.3 Carry-over and expiry

- You may carry over a maximum of **6 days** of unused vacation into the following calendar year.
- Carried-over days must be taken by 31 March of the following year. For balances from 2026 the expiry date is **31 March 2027**.
- Days above the carry-over limit are not transferred; your manager will help you schedule them during the fourth quarter.
- Exceptions (for example after long-term illness or parental leave) need written approval from People & Culture.
- Unused vacation is paid out only when employment ends.

## 3. Public holidays and company days

Employees observe the public holidays of the country in which their employment contract is registered. For contracts registered in the Czech Republic that means **13 public holidays** in 2026; the full calendar for each country is published in People Hub. If a public holiday falls on a weekend, no replacement day is granted.

If you must work on a public holiday of your home country, for example because you are travelling on business, you may take a replacement day within the following two months.

In addition, Orbitwell grants two company-wide paid days off:

- **Orbitwell Day** on Friday 4 September 2026, the anniversary of the company's founding.
- **Winter shutdown** from 28 to 31 December 2026: four additional paid days that are not deducted from your vacation balance. Customer support and on-call engineering continue according to the published rota, and anyone rostered receives the days at another time.

## 4. Sick leave

1. Inform your manager and record the absence in People Hub by **09:30** on the first day of illness.
2. You may self-certify up to **5 sick days per calendar year** without a doctor's certificate. A single self-certified absence may last at most two consecutive working days.
3. From the third consecutive working day of illness, or once your self-certified days are used up, a doctor's certificate (electronic sick note) is required.
4. During the first **14 calendar days** of an illness, Orbitwell pays 100% of your normal net pay, topping up the statutory compensation. From day 15 the statutory sickness insurance scheme applies.
5. Each employee also receives **2 paid family care days** per year to look after a sick child or relative; longer care follows the statutory carer's benefit.

## 5. Parental leave

| Leave type | Who is eligible | Duration | Pay |
|---|---|---|---|
| Birth parent leave | The employee giving birth | 20 weeks | 100% of salary (Orbitwell tops up the statutory maternity benefit) |
| Partner leave | The other parent, including same-sex partners | 4 weeks, to be taken within 9 months of the birth or adoption | 100% of salary |
| Adoption leave | The primary adopter | 20 weeks | 100% of salary |
| Extended parental leave | Either parent | Until the child's third birthday | Statutory parental allowance |

- Let People & Culture know as early as you feel comfortable, and at least two months before the planned start of leave.
- Partner leave can be split into a maximum of two blocks.
- **Phased return:** for the first month back you may work 80% of your contracted hours on full pay.
- Vacation continues to accrue during birth parent, partner and adoption leave.

## 6. Remote and hybrid work

Orbitwell is a hybrid company built around shared lab and assembly spaces, so remote work is agreed role by role.

- **Office-based roles** may work remotely up to **3 days per week**, agreed with their manager and recorded in People Hub.
- **Tuesday is the anchor day:** teams are expected on site so that planning, reviews and social time happen in person.
- **Lab, assembly and field-service roles** work remotely only by exception, for example for documentation or training days.
- **Fully remote arrangements** require approval from your director and a written contract addendum.
- **Working from another country** is limited to **15 working days per calendar year**, within the EU/EEA only, and must be approved in People Hub before you travel. Working from outside the EU/EEA is not permitted for tax and immigration reasons.
- Employees with an approved hybrid arrangement receive a **home-office allowance of €25 per month**, paid with salary, as a contribution towards electricity and internet costs.
- Remote work must follow the Information Security Policy: use only your encrypted company laptop, lock your screen when you step away, and never let family members use company devices.

## 7. Probation period

- The probation period is **4 months** for all new employees and **8 months** for employees in managerial positions.
- Your manager agrees your probation goals with you in the first week, holds a mid-probation review, and schedules a final review two weeks before the probation ends.
- During probation either party may end the employment in writing with immediate effect.
- Once probation is complete, People & Culture confirms it in writing and certain benefits become available, as described in the relevant policies.
