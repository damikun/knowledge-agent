# New Hire Onboarding Guide

**For:** new employees starting at Orbitwell Robotics HQ in Brno (other offices follow the same steps with local contacts) · **Owner:** People & Culture · **Questions:** onboarding@orbitwell.example

Welcome to Orbitwell Robotics! This guide walks you through your first week: where to go, which accounts you will get, which trainings are mandatory and who can help. Keep it open on your phone on day one.

## 1. Before your first day

- **Three working days before you start**, you receive an activation email for your **Orbitwell ID** at your personal email address. Orbitwell ID is your single sign-on account for almost every system.
- Activate the account and choose a passphrase that follows the rules in the Information Security Policy. If you can, enrol the Orbitwell Authenticator app for multi-factor authentication straight away; otherwise the IT Service Point will help you on day one.
- Bring an identity card or passport (needed for your badge and contract), your bank details for payroll and, if you have one, your health insurance card.
- Your buddy will email you during the week before you start to say hello.

### Laptop pickup

The IT Service Point in **room B2.17** is open Monday to Friday, 08:00–16:30. Bring your badge or identity card. You will receive:

- your laptop, already encrypted and enrolled in device management;
- a headset and a docking station (already set up at your desk);
- two hardware security keys, if your role requires them under the Information Security Policy.

## 4. Mandatory trainings

| Training | Who | Deadline | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| Security awareness (SEC-100) | Everyone | Within **10 working days** of your start date | Orbitwell Academy, e-learning, about 90 minutes |
| Code of Conduct and anti-bribery (COC-100) | Everyone | By the end of your first month | Orbitwell Academy |
| Data protection basics (GDPR-100) | Everyone | Within 20 working days of your start date | Orbitwell Academy |
| Lab Safety Induction (LS-101) | Anyone who needs access to the robotics lab, assembly hall or test areas | Must be passed **before lab access is granted** | Safety Training Room C1.05, every Thursday 08:30–12:30 |
| OW-X7 operator training (PRD-210) | Support, field service and application engineers | Within your first two months | Training cell, Building C |

- **Security awareness:** until SEC-100 is completed, your Orbitwell ID only gives access to email and Orbitwell Academy. Finish it early.
- **Lab Safety Induction:** LS-101 is a four-hour session with a theory part and a practical part in a robot cell, followed by a short test. Book your place through lab-safety@orbitwell.example. Your badge is activated for the lab within one working day after you pass, and the induction must be repeated every year.
- Orbitwell Academy sends reminders before each deadline. If a deadline is missed, your manager is notified automatically.

## 5. Buddy programme

- Every new hire is paired with a **buddy** from a different team. People & Culture assigns the buddy before your first day.
- The buddy programme runs for your **first 8 weeks**. Plan an informal catch-up with your buddy once a week, for example over coffee.
- Your buddy helps with the unwritten rules: who is who, which tools to use for what, where to have lunch. Your manager remains your first contact for work priorities and your probation goals.
- After one year at Orbitwell you can volunteer to become a buddy yourself by writing to buddies@orbitwell.example.
